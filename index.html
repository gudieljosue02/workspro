Common questions

Questions homeowners ask us

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Brownsville, Tennessee and work throughout Haywood County, including Bells and Stanton. We also travel to Ripley, Covington, Halls, Somerville and Jackson, roughly 30 miles out from Brownsville.

How do I get an estimate?

The fastest way is to text photos of the work to (731) 443-2368. Send a picture or two and a short description, and we'll text you back a free estimate. You can also fill out the form on this page or call us.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free and there's no obligation. You get a clear price before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

How much does a handyman cost?

It depends on the job, which is exactly why we quote each one instead of guessing. Text us a photo and we'll give you a real number for your project, in writing, before we start. No trip charge for an estimate anywhere in Haywood County.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes, small jobs are most of what we do. Mounting a TV, patching drywall, fixing a sticking door, swapping a faucet, touching up paint. If it's on your to-do list and it's been there too long, text us a photo.

How soon can you come out?

We answer texts and calls the same day during business hours and aim to reply to every quote request within one business day, usually much sooner. We'll give you a real appointment window and stick to it.

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 7am to 6pm. Texts sent after hours get answered the next morning.

What kinds of work do you do?

General handyman repairs, remodeling, interior and exterior painting, plumbing repairs, and doors and windows. If a job needs a specialty trade, we'll tell you upfront and point you to the right pro.

Do you clean up when you're done?

Always. We haul off our debris and leave your home cleaner than we found it. You shouldn't have to tidy up after the person you hired.
